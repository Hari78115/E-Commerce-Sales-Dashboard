# E-Commerce Data Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an **E-Commerce Data Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and business trends.

The dashboard transforms raw e-commerce data into meaningful visual insights and interactive reports that can support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance.
* Track revenue, orders, quantity sold, and profit.
* Identify top-performing products and categories.
* Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
* Understand sales trends over time.
* Compare performance across different regions and segments.
* Identify business areas that require improvement.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI**
* **Power Query**
* **DAX**
* **Data Cleaning & Transformation**
* **Data Modeling**
* **Data Visualization**

## 📈 Dashboard KPIs

The dashboard provides important business KPIs such as:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Total Quantity Sold
* Average Order Value
* Sales Growth
* Profit Margin

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### Sales Analysis

Analyzed sales and revenue trends across different time periods to understand overall business performance.

### Product Analysis

Identified high-performing and low-performing products and categories based on sales and profit.

### Customer Analysis

Analyzed customer purchasing patterns and contribution to overall revenue.

### Regional Analysis

Compared sales and profitability across different regions and locations.

### Time-Based Analysis

Analyzed monthly and yearly trends to identify seasonal patterns and changes in business performance.
